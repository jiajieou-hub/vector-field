## Description

Vector Field Sketchpad is a free tool that runs in your browser. Type the components of any 2D or 3D vector field and watch arrows, streamlines and particles move through it, with divergence, curl, circulation and flux computed as you go.

▶ Try it: https://jiajieou-hub.github.io/vector-field/
💻 Source code (MIT): https://github.com/jiajieou-hub/vector-field

In this video:
• Type any field, in plain math or LaTeX
• Arrows, streamlines, particles and divergence/curl heat-maps
• Probe any point for F, div F and curl F; a paddle wheel shows the curl
• Phase portraits: drag a parameter and watch a Hopf limit cycle appear
• Draw a loop to get circulation and flux, checked against Green's theorem
• Step-by-step demos of Green's, Stokes' and the divergence theorem
• 3D fields and strange attractors: the Lorenz and Halvorsen attractors, a magnetic dipole, the field of a current-carrying wire

Made for students and teachers of multivariable calculus, differential equations, electromagnetism and fluid mechanics. The interface is in English and 中文.

Feedback is welcome: open an issue on GitHub or email jiajie_ou@berkeley.edu.
